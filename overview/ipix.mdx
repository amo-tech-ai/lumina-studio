iPix is the operator studio for fashion brands. You do not start by reading architecture. You start by bringing in a real website — the same way a studio would pull a client's lookbook before planning a shoot.

This page is the first journey: **login → `/app` → brand URL → Brand Hub crawl → Brand DNA**.

<Tip>
  If you only remember one path: sign in, open Brand Hub, add the brand website, wait until the DNA scores appear on the brand card.
</Tip>

## What you will have when this works

Maya at Lumina Studio can paste `https://maisonnoir.com`, watch the crawl, and see Brand DNA on that brand in Brand Hub. Then she can plan a shoot against that profile instead of guessing the look.

## First journey

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open `/login` and sign in. iPix uses Supabase auth (PKCE). After a successful login you should land in the operator app, not the public marketing site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the operator app">
    Go to `/app`. This is Command Center — the home for the signed-in operator. From here you move into Brand Hub, shoots, planner, and CRM. If you are not signed in, `/app` will send you back toward login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Brand Hub">
    Go to `/app/brand`. This is the brand list. Empty state copy: add a brand and iPix will crawl it and build Brand DNA.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give iPix the brand URL">
    Choose **Add brand**. That opens onboarding (`/onboarding`, also reachable as `/app/onboarding`). The website field is required — Brand DNA is crawl-backed. Paste a real site such as `https://maisonnoir.com`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the crawl, then read Brand DNA">
    Status text includes **Crawling website…** while `start-brand-crawl` runs. When analysis finishes, Brand Hub shows the DNA score and pillars on the brand card. Open the brand (`/app/brand/[id]`) to work from that profile. Onboarding also has a Brand DNA payoff screen with **Return to Brand Hub**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If something fails

- **No brands, or you cannot see another org's brands** — expected. Row Level Security scopes brands to your organization.
- **Crawl does not start** — the brand needs a valid `https://…` URL. Restart from Brand Hub if analysis failed.
- **Chat `/info` 403 after login** — that is CopilotKit org membership, not this Brand Hub path. Fix membership; do not weaken auth.
